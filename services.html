Standard Cleaning

Our standard clean is the backbone of what we do — and the service most of our recurring clients rely on week after week. It covers every main living area, your kitchen, all bathrooms, and the high-traffic zones that accumulate dust and grime fastest. It's designed to keep your home consistently clean so you never have to worry about it.

Every standard clean is performed with care by the same assigned team members who will get to know your home's layout, your preferences, and any quirks worth knowing. We bring all our own supplies and equipment — just let us in and we'll take it from there.

Standard cleaning is ideal for busy households that want professional-level cleanliness maintained on a regular schedule without the hassle of managing it themselves.

  • Dusting all accessible surfaces, shelves, and furniture
  • Vacuuming carpets, rugs, and upholstered furniture
  • Mopping all hard floors
  • Scrubbing toilets, sinks, tubs, and shower surfaces
  • Wiping down kitchen counters, stovetop, and exterior of appliances
  • Emptying interior trash cans and replacing liners

Deep Cleaning

A deep clean goes beyond your standard visit to tackle every corner, crevice, and surface that doesn't get regular attention. We recommend a deep clean for first-time clients, after a long period without professional cleaning, or as a seasonal reset to bring your home back to its best condition.

Our team will spend additional time on the areas that accumulate the most buildup — inside your oven, under furniture, along baseboards, and behind appliances. The result is a level of cleanliness that feels genuinely different when you walk through the door.

Many of our recurring clients schedule a deep clean at the start of their service and then maintain that freshness with regular standard visits throughout the year.

  • Cleaning inside the oven and microwave
  • Wiping down baseboards throughout the home
  • Cleaning ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • Cleaning inside all cabinets and drawers
  • Scrubbing grout lines in tile areas
  • Cleaning behind and underneath appliances
  • Washing window sills and interior window tracks
  • Detailed bathroom scrubbing including showerhead and fixtures

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Moving is stressful enough without worrying about whether the last tenant left the place clean — or whether you'll lose your security deposit because you didn't. Our move cleaning service is designed specifically for the transition moments in life, and we know exactly what landlords and property managers look for.

Whether you're moving out of a rental and need to leave it spotless, or moving into a new home and want a fresh start before your furniture arrives, we'll make sure every surface is cleaned to the standard it deserves. We clean everything from the inside of the refrigerator to the top of the door frames.

Our move clients consistently report getting their full security deposit back and feeling confident handing over keys. We're happy to work around your moving schedule, including weekends.

  • Full deep clean of all rooms, closets, and storage spaces
  • Cleaning inside and outside of all appliances
  • Wiping down all cabinet interiors and shelving
  • Cleaning inside the refrigerator and freezer
  • Scrubbing bathrooms top to bottom including grout

Post-Construction Cleaning

Renovation dust is unlike anything else — it's fine, it gets into every crack, and it settles back down just when you think you've gotten it all. Our post-construction cleaning service is specifically designed to get your home livable again after any construction project, large or small.

We handle everything from the visible drywall dust on surfaces to the hidden debris tucked into vents and corners. Our team uses specialized equipment and techniques to clear construction-grade messes without damaging freshly finished surfaces, new flooring, or recently installed fixtures.

We work with homeowners, contractors, and property managers across Wilmington and the surrounding area. If you have a project finishing up and need the space cleaned before move-in or handover, we can typically schedule within a few days of completion.

  • Removing all construction dust from surfaces, floors, and ceilings
  • Cleaning new fixtures, hardware, and appliances
  • Washing windows and removing any adhesive labels or stickers
  • Vacuuming and mopping all floors including beneath cabinets
  • Cleaning inside cabinets and newly installed shelving
  • Wiping down all painted surfaces, trim, and baseboards
